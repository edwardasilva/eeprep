A timeline from Year 1 to after graduation. Year 1: join a design team or lab, start a list of everything you build, and walk one career fair. Year 2: a one-page resume, fall and spring career fairs, a note to a professor, and summer research if eligible. Year 3: the main internship push, STAR stories, and an internship with a question about a return offer. Year 4: full-time applications and comparing offers. After graduation, test and technician roles are a way in. A band shows the busiest recruiting months at many schools, September to October and January to February. A second band tells F-1 students that CPT needs one full academic year first, that OPT can be filed up to 90 days before they finish, and to ask their DSO before they work.
