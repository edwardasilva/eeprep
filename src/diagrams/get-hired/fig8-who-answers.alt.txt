A flow chart that starts with "What is your question?" and splits three ways. If you ask whether you can work and when, on F-1, CPT, or OPT, ask your school's DSO, and get authorization before you start. If a posting says "U.S. person" or ITAR, ask the employer's recruiter. The term is defined in 22 CFR 120.62. If a posting needs a security clearance, the employer's security officer handles it after a written offer, and you cannot apply for a clearance yourself. A footer says eeprep does not answer these questions, and these offices do.
