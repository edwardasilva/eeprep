These are the repositories and lists this site leans on. Each one is free, and none of them pays eeprep. A list finds the role, and you apply on the employer's page.

## Job lists on GitHub

- [SimplifyJobs's internship list](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships) is updated daily by Simplify and Pitt CSC. On 9 November 2026 it held 1,612 roles, 282 of them hardware. Closed roles get a lock and move to an inactive page.
- [SimplifyJobs's new-grad list](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/New-Grad-Positions) is the same format for full-time roles. It held 601 roles that day, 119 of them hardware. The [[Job boards|job boards page]] says how these lists get their data.

## Resumes

- [The r/EngineeringResumes wiki](https://www.reddit.com/r/EngineeringResumes/wiki/index/) is the source for the rules on the [[Resume|resume page]]. Read the whole thing once.
- [Its template page](https://www.reddit.com/r/EngineeringResumes/wiki/templates/) has a Google Docs template and a LaTeX one.
- [resume-generator](https://github.com/edwardasilva/resume-generator) turns one JSON file into a one-page PDF with the layout from the Resume page. The [[Walkthrough|walkthrough page]] shows the five example files it ships with.
- [Georgia Tech's 2026 cover letter guide](https://c14750.wpmucdn.com/5568/files/2026/10/Cover-Letter-Guide26.pdf) explains when to send a cover letter and how to write one, with undergraduate examples.

## Courses and references

- [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/about/) publishes materials from more than 2,500 courses. The [[Forever learning#Take free courses|section on taking free courses]] on the Forever learning page names the three that cover the core of an EE degree.
- [NCEES](https://ncees.org/licensure/) has the FE and PE exam rules. The [[Forever learning#The FE exam and the PE license|section on the FE exam and the PE license]] on the Forever learning page has the path.
- [O\*NET's electrical engineering page](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00) lists the titles employers use. Search postings by those titles, not by "electrical engineer".

## Pay

- [The BLS page on electrical and electronics engineers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm) has the federal medians and the outlook.
- [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/) has reported pay by company and level.
- [NACE's compensation page](https://www.naceweb.org/job-market/compensation/) has the new-graduate starting-salary summaries. The [[Help#Pay and offers|pay and offers]] section on the Help page says how far to trust each one.
