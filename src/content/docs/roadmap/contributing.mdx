eeprep is open source. Contributions and advice are welcome from engineers, engineering managers, recruiters, and leadership. If you hire electrical engineers or work as one, you can correct what these pages get wrong.

## Send advice

Open an issue with one of the [eeprep issue forms](https://github.com/edwardasilva/eeprep/issues/new/choose). Name the page, say what you would change, and say why. Link a source if you have one. Every claim on eeprep links to its source, and a claim that can't be backed up stays off the site.

## Change the site

<ChangeFlowFigure />

Every change starts as an issue. Then cut a short branch from `main` and open a pull request. Keep it to one page or one fix, about 50 to 300 changed lines. The [contribution guide](https://github.com/edwardasilva/eeprep/blob/main/.github/CONTRIBUTING.md) has the checks to run and the local commands.

- Guide pages are Markdown and MDX files in `src/content/docs`. Every outside link needs an entry in `src/content/references.bib`.
- Figures are components in `src/components`. Their words and drawings count as content.
- Site code, such as plugins, styles, and scripts, is in `src/` and `.github/scripts`.
- [resume-generator](https://github.com/edwardasilva/resume-generator) is a separate repo with its own pull requests and releases.

## Report a security problem

Do not open a public issue. A public issue shows the problem to every reader. Follow the [security policy](https://github.com/edwardasilva/eeprep/blob/main/.github/SECURITY.md) to report it in private.
