Most of this guide leans toward embedded work, because that is my field. [The Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) says electrical and electronics engineers may specialize in an area such as electric power, radio communications, or control systems](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). This page covers eleven electrical engineering (EE) paths and how to test one before you commit. Suggestions here without a source are mine.

## TLDR;

- Test a path with the project in its section, then with an internship or co-op.
- Read each posting's degree line, because some chip design and verification roles ask for a master's or more.
- If power is your goal, take the Fundamentals of Engineering (FE) exam near graduation.
- If you are still deciding in your final year, apply to rotation programs.

## The eleven paths at a glance

The first five paths span many industries, and the last six sit mostly at chip and electronics companies. Digital design and verification target field-programmable gate arrays (FPGAs), chips you can reconfigure, and application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs), chips built for one job.

| Path                                      | Titles to search                                                                           | Degree usually expected                                        |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Embedded and firmware                     | Embedded software engineer, firmware engineer                                              | Bachelor's                                                     |
| Power                                     | Electrical engineer, electrical project engineer, utility rotation programs                | Bachelor's                                                     |
| Analog and mixed-signal                   | Integrated circuit (IC) design engineer, product engineer                                  | Bachelor's, sometimes a master's for chip design               |
| Radio frequency (RF) and communications   | RF engineer                                                                                | Bachelor's, sometimes a PhD for RF chip design                 |
| Controls                                  | Electrical controls engineer, automation engineer                                          | Bachelor's                                                     |
| Digital design and FPGA                   | Hardware design engineer, electrical design engineer (FPGA/ASIC), physical design engineer | Bachelor's or master's                                         |
| Design verification                       | ASIC verification engineer, design verification engineer                                   | Bachelor's for internships, a master's on one new-grad posting |
| Test, validation, and product engineering | Test engineer, product engineer, validation engineer                                       | Bachelor's                                                     |
| Semiconductor process and yield           | Process integration engineer, yield enhancement engineer                                   | Bachelor's in EE, materials, or a related field                |
| Applications engineering                  | Applications engineer, field applications engineer                                         | Varies by employer, so read each posting                       |
| Board-level hardware design               | Electronics design engineer, hardware engineer                                             | Bachelor's                                                     |

The chip design notes in the analog and RF rows each rest on one online comment, quoted in the [[Graduate study#Which fields expect one|section on which fields expect one]] on the graduate study page.

## How to choose and test a path

Start with your courses. Take electives for both breadth and depth, and pick ones you can explain in detail, because interviewers ask about coursework. [Illinois's electrical and computer engineering (ECE) department says students need to strike a balance between the two](https://ece.illinois.edu/academics/ugrad/subdisciplines). [Students there will likely take courses listed under several areas, and the bachelor's diploma does not record a specialization](https://ece.illinois.edu/academics/ugrad/subdisciplines), so there a wrong elective never reaches the diploma. Schools group electives under different headings, so look under more than one.

Next, ask several engineers in each path for an informational interview, a short talk about their work. [MIT's career office calls it a low-pressure way to gather career information and refine your goals](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/conducting-informational-interviews/). [It warns against relying too heavily on the views of only one or two people](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/conducting-informational-interviews/). The [[Networking#The informational interview|section on the informational interview]] on the networking page shows how to ask.

An internship or co-op is the closest test, because you do the real work for a summer or a semester. [UT Austin's Cockrell School lists exploring career interests and assessing interest in specific engineering fields among an internship's benefits](https://cockrell.utexas.edu/student-life/career-services/co-ops-and-internships/). [A Drexel ECE student from the class of 2019 expected co-op to let them try out different fields and decide for sure what they liked](https://drexel.edu/engineering/academics/departments/electrical-computer-engineering/academic-programs/department-co-ops). [The BLS says employers also value internships and cooperative engineering programs](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

After graduation, a rotation program moves you through several teams, with pay. [GE Aerospace's Edison program has 3 to 4 rotations over 2 years](https://careers.geaerospace.com/global/en/edison-engineering-development-program), in [hardware design, controls, validation testing, power electronics, and software](https://careers.geaerospace.com/global/en/edison-engineering-development-program). [Schweitzer Engineering Laboratories (SEL) says its 18-month rotation through three areas makes you a strong candidate for the career path you choose](https://selinc.com/company/careers/engineer-rotation-program/). The power section covers SEL's program and PG&E's. PG&E, a California utility, looks for students who are graduating, so apply in your final year.

Treat your first job as one try of several. [In November 2016, the State Science & Technology Institute (SSTI) reported on a study of trained scientists and engineers](https://ssti.org/blog/engineering-job-mismatch-spurs-study). [More than 40 percent worked outside their field of study or in one only somewhat related, and most of those outside it chose to be](https://ssti.org/blog/engineering-job-mismatch-spurs-study). The study covers whole fields, not EE subfields, and it measures work outside the field studied, not job changes.

[Texas Instruments (TI) tells candidates to be ready to discuss every detail on the resume, including tools, design steps, and results](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/recruitment-process). [Its technical questions may include coding problems, circuit analysis, or design scenarios](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/recruitment-process). [Micron says its technical questions typically focus on your completed coursework and problem-solving abilities](https://www.micron.com/about/careers/university-recruiting). The [[How to interview|how to interview page]] covers how to prepare.

## Embedded and firmware

Embedded engineers write firmware, the code that runs on a microcontroller (a small computer on one chip), so the work sits where circuit meets software. [UT Austin's embedded systems lab covers interfacing from both the hardware and the software side](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~valvano/EE445L/EE445L_SPRING_2025.htm). If you like reading a datasheet and then making a timer or serial port do what it says, this is the path.

Search for embedded software engineer, my own title. [One recruiter in aerospace and defense wrote on Reddit that embedded software engineers are probably the hardest and most expensive hires to find in that industry](https://www.reddit.com/r/EngineeringResumes/comments/1sj2rgt/student_landed_nvidia_offer_sophomore_cs_major_at/). [Chipmaker TI's software engineers work on system architectures and embedded programming](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering), and [a 2022 list of NVIDIA new-grad hardware roles that Purdue ECE posted included firmware](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ECE/Academics/Graduates/Announcements/20220408_NVIDIA_Jobs).

For a project, follow the [[Software#Embedded firmware|embedded firmware]] section on the software page. It goes from bare-metal code, with no operating system, to a real-time one that meets timing deadlines. For courses, [Georgia Tech's ECE school files embedded systems and hardware-software codesign under very-large-scale integration (VLSI) systems and digital design](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[The BLS says electrical and electronics engineers typically need at least a bachelor's degree in a related engineering field](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm), and [entry-level jobs do not require a license](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

For interviews, start with the fundamentals, which Wind River, an embedded software company, [says come up often when an applicant has little experience](https://www.windriver.com/blog/Internal-Job-Interview-Questions-for-Embedded-Systems-Developers). [Its January 2026 blog post for embedded developers looking for work asks about the heap and the stack, and about interrupt handlers](https://www.windriver.com/blog/Internal-Job-Interview-Questions-for-Embedded-Systems-Developers). [It also covers real-time operating system tasks, mutexes versus semaphores, priority inversion, and when to poll a register instead of using an interrupt](https://www.windriver.com/blog/Internal-Job-Interview-Questions-for-Embedded-Systems-Developers). The [[How to interview#A note on embedded interviews|note on embedded interviews]] on the how to interview page shows how to trace one project from the pin to the code.

To start, read [the suggested text for UT Austin's ECE 445L in spring 2025, Embedded Systems: Real-Time Interfacing to ARM Cortex M Microcontrollers](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~valvano/EE445L/EE445L_SPRING_2025.htm). [TI made its Precision Labs videos to accelerate the development of engineers early in their career](https://www.ti.com/video/series/precision-labs.html), and [the microcontroller series covers the CPU core, timers, memory, and programming](https://www.ti.com/video/series/precision-labs.html).

## Power

Power suits someone who liked three-phase circuits and wants work on real equipment, some of it in the field. Power electronics, such as converters, belongs here too, and its chip side, power-management integrated circuits, sits under analog.

O\*NET, a US Department of Labor job database, [lists tasks for electrical engineers such as fixing power system problems](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00) and [developing systems that make electricity from wind, solar, or biofuels](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). [SEL's Engineering Services group gives customers expertise on power system dynamics, protection, automation, and control, from planning through commissioning](https://selinc.com/company/careers/engineer-rotation-program/), when a system goes into service.

Search for electrical engineer, electrical design engineer, and electrical project engineer, [three of O\*NET's reported titles for electrical engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). At PG&E, [the Engineer Rotation Development Program in electric engineering gives 12 months in up to two departments, in Sacramento, Fresno, or the Bay Area](https://jobs.pge.com/university-programs). [SEL's Engineer Rotation Program runs 18 months through three areas, such as research and development, engineering services, and manufacturing](https://selinc.com/company/careers/engineer-rotation-program/).

For a project, build the converter in the [[Hardware#Hardware builds that count|hardware builds that count]] section on the hardware page. For courses, [Georgia Tech's electrical energy group covers power system protection and power electronics, plus motors, generators, and power conversion](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

Take a power systems elective if you can, because [PG&E's ideal candidates are graduating with a bachelor's or master's in electrical engineering, ideally with an emphasis in power systems](https://jobs.pge.com/university-programs).

[The BLS counted engineering services as the largest employer of electrical engineers in 2025, at 22 percent, with electric power generation, transmission, and distribution at 10 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm), so search both. [SEL offers year-round electrical engineering internships, many at its headquarters in Pullman, Washington](https://selinc.com/company/careers/sel-internships/).

To start, join IEEE as a student, then the IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES). [PES adds \$1.00 a year at the Essential level, with the first year free](https://ieee-pes.org/membership-overview/student-members/). [Its student members get three PES scholarships to apply for, plus student support and poster contests at PES conferences](https://ieee-pes.org/membership-overview/student-members/).

After your circuits and electronics courses, work through [MIT's 6.622 Power Electronics from Spring 2023, a graduate course whose prerequisite is 6.2500 Nanoelectronics and Computing Systems](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-622-power-electronics-spring-2023/pages/syllabus/). [Its text is Principles of Power Electronics, from Cambridge University Press in 2023](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-622-power-electronics-spring-2023/pages/syllabus/).

If power is your goal, take the FE exam near graduation. [The National Council of Examiners for Engineering and Surveying (NCEES) says the FE is generally the first step toward a professional engineer (PE) license](https://ncees.org/exams/fe-exam/). [It designed the exam for recent graduates and students close to finishing an accredited engineering degree](https://ncees.org/exams/fe-exam/). The license comes later. [The Principles and Practice of Engineering (PE) exam for power, given year-round, is designed for engineers with at least four years of work after college](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).

The FE can also show up in postings for your second job. [A Burns & McDonnell posting open in November 2026 put a staff-level substation protection engineer under a licensed professional engineer's supervision](https://burnsmcd.jobs/washington-dc/staff-substation-pc-engineer-transmission-distribution-mid-atlantic-region/7D486F7994A24FC7A3B6BE7F9643D31F/job/). [It asked for 3 years of experience, and preferred a passed FE, an engineer-in-training (EIT) designation, or a PE](https://burnsmcd.jobs/washington-dc/staff-substation-pc-engineer-transmission-distribution-mid-atlantic-region/7D486F7994A24FC7A3B6BE7F9643D31F/job/).

## Analog and mixed-signal

Analog suits you if you like reasoning through a circuit one transistor at a time, then checking it on the bench. Mixed-signal chips put analog and digital circuits together. At TI, [design engineers in analog, digital, mixed-signal, and RF define, design, model, and verify integrated circuits](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). Search for IC design engineer, product engineer, and test engineer, [three of O\*NET's reported titles for electronics engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00).

[Analog Devices lists intern projects such as designing a high-voltage, high-output-current operational amplifier (op amp) and evaluating an on-chip buffer for a new analog-to-digital converter (ADC)](https://www.analog.com/en/careers/student-resources.html). For a project, simulate an op amp or ADC front end in LTspice, then build and measure it. For courses, [Georgia Tech files analog electronics, high-speed mixed-signal systems, and power-management integrated circuits under electronic design and applications](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[In O\*NET's survey of electronics engineers, 83 percent said a new hire needs a bachelor's, and 3 percent a master's](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00). The [[Graduate study#Which fields expect one|section on which fields expect one]] on the graduate study page quotes an engineer who said analog chip design required a master's. Another, who hires analog designers, valued a year of experience more.

[The BLS counted semiconductor and other electronic component manufacturing among the largest employers of electronics engineers in 2025, at 14 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). [Analog Devices says it hires over 500 interns around the globe and converts over 60 percent of them to full-time employees after graduation](https://www.analog.com/en/careers/student-resources.html).

For interviews, the [[How to interview#Fundamentals from the ground up|fundamentals]] section on the how to interview page lists analog questions, such as op-amp gain and a buck converter versus a low-dropout regulator.

To start, work through [MIT's 6.002 Circuits and Electronics from Spring 2007, an undergraduate first course in EE](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/), with [its text, Foundations of Analog and Digital Electronic Circuits](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/). [TI's Precision Labs also has series on amplifiers and on analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog converters](https://www.ti.com/video/series/precision-labs.html).

For a contest, try [the IEEE Solid-State Circuits Society (SSCS) Student Circuit Contest, open to undergraduate and graduate students with no IEEE or SSCS membership needed](https://sscs.ieee.org/education/sscs-student-circuit-contest/). [An entry is a two-page answer from one student or a team of two, and up to three winners get \$500](https://sscs.ieee.org/education/sscs-student-circuit-contest/). [Every valid entry earns a free year of SSCS membership, and the 2026 deadline was May 31](https://sscs.ieee.org/education/sscs-student-circuit-contest/), so check for the next one.

For a license, [the closest PE exam is Electronics, Controls, and Communications, given once a year](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).

## RF and communications

RF engineers work on the circuits and antennas that send and receive radio signals, so pick RF if you enjoyed electromagnetics. [Analog Devices lists intern projects such as moving an RF modulator design to a new process and writing software that automates the evaluation of voltage-controlled oscillators](https://www.analog.com/en/careers/student-resources.html). Search for RF engineer, [which O\*NET lists among the reported titles for electronics engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00).

An amateur radio (ham) license gets you transmitting before any internship. [The Federal Communications Commission (FCC) says most new amateur radio operators start at the Technician class, after an exam given by volunteer examiners](https://www.fcc.gov/wireless/bureau-divisions/mobility-division/amateur-radio-service). For courses, [Georgia Tech's electromagnetics group covers antenna design and satellite communications, plus radar, communication, and navigation](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

For RF chip design, the [[Graduate study#Which fields expect one|graduate study page]] quotes a Reddit commenter who said many RF design postings require a PhD.

Beyond chipmakers, [the BLS listed the federal government at 15 percent and telecommunications at 14 percent among the largest employers of electronics engineers in 2025](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

For interviews, review Smith charts, transmission lines, and impedance matching. [The unnamed author of antenna-theory.com, who has given and taken antenna engineer interviews, calls all three fair game](https://www.antenna-theory.com/careers/interview.php). [The sample questions ask you to match an impedance on a Smith chart with lumped parts and to explain a quarter-wavelength transformer](https://www.antenna-theory.com/careers/interview.php). The page is written for antenna roles, so review your RF circuits course too.

To start, work through [MIT's 6.013 Electromagnetics and Applications from Spring 2009, an undergraduate course with an open textbook and problem sets with solutions](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-013-electromagnetics-and-applications-spring-2009/). Pozar's Microwave Engineering comes next. [Illinois's ECE 457, Microwave Devices and Circuits, assigned the 4th edition in fall 2025, with ECE 340 and ECE 350 as prerequisites](https://courses.grainger.illinois.edu/ece457/fa2025/).

[The International Microwave Symposium (IMS) invites eligible students to its student design competitions, with cash prizes for the winning teams](https://ims-ieee.org/students). [Its Project Connect travel grants bring selected undergraduate and first-year graduate students to the conference](https://ims-ieee.org/students). Its competition rules still refer to IMS 2026, so check the current year.

For a license, [the closest PE exam is Electronics, Controls, and Communications, as with analog](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).

## Controls

If you liked feedback and stability in class, controls puts them on a machine. [O\*NET's tasks for electrical engineers include developing software to control electrical systems](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). [O\*NET also lists programmable logic controller (PLC) and supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) software among their tools](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00).

Search for electrical controls engineer, [an O\*NET reported title for electrical engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00), and for automation engineer, control systems engineer, and process controls engineer. [O\*NET reports those three for mechatronics engineers, who design engineering systems that automate industrial tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.05).

For a project, close a loop on real hardware, such as a motor held at a set speed. Show the tuned step response, how the speed settles after the set point jumps. [TI's Precision Labs has a motor driver series on brushed-DC, stepper, and brushless-DC motors](https://www.ti.com/video/series/precision-labs.html). For courses, [Georgia Tech's systems and controls group covers mathematical and computational techniques for modeling, estimation, and control](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[In O\*NET's survey of mechatronics engineers, 66 percent said a new hire needs a bachelor's, 21 percent some college, and 10 percent an associate's degree](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.05).

[O\*NET's top industries for electrical engineers are manufacturing, and professional, scientific, and technical services](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00).

To start, take your school's undergraduate controls course. Then go further with [MIT's 6.302 Feedback Systems from Spring 2007, a graduate course on stability, root locus, the Nyquist criterion, and compensation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-302-feedback-systems-spring-2007/).

For a license, [NCEES gives a separate PE Control Systems exam, computer-based and offered once a year, and the next one is April 14, 2027](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/control-systems/).

## Digital design and FPGA

Digital designers describe logic in a hardware description language (HDL), and tools turn that code into gates. The work fits people who think in clock cycles and like code that becomes a circuit. [O\*NET's tasks for computer hardware engineers include designing CPUs and building and testing prototypes with computer simulation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2061.00).

[A Micron new-grad posting in Boise, closed as of November 2026, was for a role that built FPGA and ASIC designs for Micron's chip manufacturing test equipment](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/30512424). [The engineer would write HDL modules and debug register-transfer level (RTL) code in simulation, then run synthesis, place and route, and static timing analysis](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/30512424).

Search for hardware design engineer, hardware engineer, and physical design engineer, [three of O\*NET's reported titles for computer hardware engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2061.00). [A 2022 list of NVIDIA new-grad roles that Purdue ECE posted also named ASIC design and hardware design](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ECE/Academics/Graduates/Announcements/20220408_NVIDIA_Jobs).

For a project, build your own design in Verilog on an FPGA, as [students in MIT's 6.111, an undergraduate digital lab from Spring 2006, do for their final project](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-111-introductory-digital-systems-laboratory-spring-2006/). Show its testbench, the code that checks it. The [[Software#FPGA and HDL|section on FPGA and HDL]] on the software page lists free simulators. The FPGA equalizer by Alex Doe, the example student, is a fixed-point digital signal processing (DSP) build that suits a student who liked signals and systems. Its resume lines are under [[Hardware#Hardware builds that count|hardware builds that count]] on the hardware page.

For courses, [NVIDIA's 2027 hardware ASIC design internship lists course or internship experience in RTL design, VLSI design, static timing analysis, and synthesis](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397025803). [Georgia Tech files both computer architecture and FPGAs and reconfigurable computing under computer systems and software](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[That NVIDIA internship takes students working toward a bachelor's, master's, or PhD in EE, computer engineering, or a related field](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397025803). [Micron's posting asked for a bachelor's or master's in electrical engineering or a related discipline](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/30512424). But [in O\*NET's survey of computer hardware engineers, 48 percent said a new hire needs a bachelor's and 34 percent a master's](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2061.00), far above the 3 percent for electronics engineers.

Physical design, the back half of the chip design flow, turns the logic into a chip layout. It suits someone who likes tools and scripts as much as logic. [NVIDIA's 2027 physical design internship lists floorplanning, place and route, static timing analysis, power integrity, physical verification, and clock tree synthesis](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397026661), and [takes bachelor's, master's, and PhD students too](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397026661). For courses, [Georgia Tech files design automation for physical design under VLSI systems and digital design](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[The BLS counted semiconductor and other electronic component manufacturing as the largest employer of computer hardware engineers in 2025, at 24 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/computer-hardware-engineers.htm). [Research and development came next at 18 percent, then computer systems design at 15 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/computer-hardware-engineers.htm). [The BLS put the May 2025 median pay for computer hardware engineers at \$161,740](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/computer-hardware-engineers.htm), compared with [\$125,040 for electrical and electronics engineers combined](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). Both medians cover every experience level, so neither is starting pay.

For interviews, [the Chip Insights newsletter by Bharath Suresh tells digital design candidates to work out both a Mealy and a Moore state machine for any scenario](https://chipinsights.net/p/hardware-engineering-interview-resources). [It also lists the synthesizable Verilog constructs, setup and hold time, and metastability](https://chipinsights.net/p/hardware-engineering-interview-resources). [An unsigned topic list on The Data Bus, a chip design site, says every digital design interview is bound to have questions on counters](https://thedatabus.in/interview_list/). [It says a new graduate must know the basic clock domain crossing problem, even without having worked on it](https://thedatabus.in/interview_list/). Clock domain crossing means passing a signal between two clocks. For Verilog practice, the [[How to interview#Fundamentals from the ground up|fundamentals]] section on the how to interview page points to HDLBits.

To start, take a course like 6.111. For a team project, [the IEEE Circuits and Systems Society (CASS) Student Design Competition takes teams of 3 to 10 undergraduates with a faculty or graduate student mentor](https://ieee-cas.org/society-involvement/student-design-competition). [At least three team members must belong to CASS, and 2027 phase one registration runs from November 2, 2026, to January 11, 2027](https://ieee-cas.org/society-involvement/student-design-competition). [CASS reimburses travel to the ISCAS 2027 conference for the four finalists, and the final's winning team gets a \$3,000 prize](https://ieee-cas.org/society-involvement/student-design-competition).

For a license, [the closest PE exam is Computer Engineering, given once a year and designed for engineers with at least four years of work after college](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/). [In the pass-rate table NCEES updated in January 2026, only 27 first-time takers took it](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).

## Design verification

Verification engineers check a digital design before it becomes hardware, by [writing tests for digital logic modules that cover all the different use cases, as Chip Insights describes it](https://chipinsights.net/p/hardware-engineering-interview-resources). The work suits you if you like writing software and finding the bug someone else missed.

[NVIDIA's closed 2026 new-grad verification posting in Austin had the engineer turn the architecture specification into a test plan and carry it out](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893392585259). [The engineer would build Universal Verification Methodology (UVM) components, sequences, tests, and scoreboards, then launch regressions and resolve the issues](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893392585259). A regression is a full rerun of the test suite.

Search for design verification engineer, ASIC verification, formal verification, and GPU design verification, [the last three from a 2022 list of NVIDIA new-grad roles that Purdue ECE posted](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ECE/Academics/Graduates/Announcements/20220408_NVIDIA_Jobs).

For a project, verify a small block of your own, such as a first-in, first-out (FIFO) buffer. Write a test plan, add random tests, and report functional coverage, the share of the plan the tests reached. [NVIDIA's 2027 ASIC design internship lists testbench creation, functional coverage, and object-oriented programming among the course areas it may require](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397025803). The [[Software#FPGA and HDL|section on FPGA and HDL]] on the software page lists free testbench tools.

Read each posting's degree line, because the postings differ. [NVIDIA's new-grad verification posting asked for a master's or higher in computer or electrical engineering, or equivalent experience](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893392585259). [Its ASIC design internship, though, takes students working toward a bachelor's, master's, or PhD](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397025803). [Micron's FPGA and ASIC posting, which included writing verification test plans, asked for a bachelor's or master's](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/30512424). With a bachelor's, aim for the internship first, because [NVIDIA calls its internships its primary pipeline for new college graduates](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/careers/university-recruiting/).

For interviews, prepare the digital design topics above too, since [the Chip Insights newsletter says verification interviews are hard because you are expected to also be a good designer](https://chipinsights.net/p/hardware-engineering-interview-resources). [It advises knowing one scripting language well, and says Python works for entry-level interviews](https://chipinsights.net/p/hardware-engineering-interview-resources). [It adds that many SystemVerilog constructs are object-oriented, and that inheritance and polymorphism are usually favorite interview topics](https://chipinsights.net/p/hardware-engineering-interview-resources). The [[How to interview#Fundamentals from the ground up|fundamentals section]] on the how to interview page links a list of design and verification interview questions.

To start, take the digital design course above, because a testbench checks a design you have to read first.

## Test, validation, and product engineering

If you like working at the lab bench, start here. At TI, [validation engineers build the hardware, software, and test methods for in-depth bench characterization of integrated circuits](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). [The job includes finding a part's limits and working out methods to break it and understand how it fails](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). [TI's test engineers build automated production tests, and its product engineers drive qualification and a manufacturing strategy](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). Qualification clears a part for production.

Beyond the table's titles, search for [compatibility test engineer and evaluation engineer, from O\*NET's reported titles for electronics engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00). The [[Hardware#Test or technician roles as a way in|section on test or technician roles]] on the hardware page has more. [TI's test or validation engineering internship, posted in September 2026, places interns as validation and characterization, product, or test engineers, depending on their degree](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/job/25017578/). [Its duties include writing production and characterization test specifications and designing interface and measurement circuits for testers](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/job/25017578/).

For a project, write a lab script that runs an instrument and fails a part that is out of spec. [The internship prefers students who can use DC power supplies, oscilloscopes, and multimeters, and who know LabVIEW, MATLAB, or C++](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/job/25017578/).

For courses, [the internship also prefers semiconductor processing, process control, or device failure analysis](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/job/25017578/). [Georgia Tech files VLSI testing and the design and test of systems on chips under VLSI systems and digital design](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[The internship, in Dallas, Tucson, Knoxville, and Phoenix, takes undergraduate or graduate students in EE, ECE, or a related field, with a GPA of 3.0 or higher](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/job/25017578/).

To start, read the [[Software#Test automation and lab scripts|section on test automation and lab scripts]] on the software page, which shows such a script. The [[Hardware#Bench skills come first|section on bench skills]] on the hardware page covers the instruments.

## Semiconductor process and yield

Process and yield engineers work in and around the fab, the plant where chips are made. They find out why wafers and parts fail and fix the cause to raise yield, the share of chips that work. [TI's manufacturing engineers lead process optimization to meet fab cycle time goals and improve yield](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). The work suits someone who liked device physics and statistics as much as circuits.

[A Micron new-grad process integration posting in Boise, closed as of November 2026, had the engineer own a process module](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [The engineer would run root cause investigations and design of experiments (DOE), and use statistical process control (SPC) and failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [A second Micron new-grad posting, open in November 2026, has a yield enhancement engineer run electrical failure analysis and fault isolation](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42813179). [The failures come from production test, qualification, and customer returns of high bandwidth memory](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42813179).

Also search for manufacturing engineer, and check each posting for shift work and travel. [The process integration posting warned of a shifted schedule and international travel of 3 months or more for technology transfer](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708), and [the yield posting mentions swing or night shifts](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42813179).

For a project, run a designed experiment on a real lab process, with the statistics in JMP, a statistics program. [The process integration posting listed familiarity with DOE, SPC, and JMP among its minimum qualifications](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708).

For courses, [the process integration posting preferred academic or internship exposure to transistors and metallization processes, yield ramp, and fabrication processes](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [Georgia Tech's electronic design and applications area focuses on device and integrated circuit fabrication](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[The process integration posting's minimum was a bachelor's in materials science, electrical, electronics, chemical, or a related engineering field](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [It preferred a master's or PhD and an internship in a 300 mm semiconductor environment](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [The yield posting takes a bachelor's or master's in EE, materials science, computer engineering, physics, or chemistry](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42813179).

In a 2023 report, the Semiconductor Industry Association, the US chip industry's trade group, [projected that by 2030, the industry would add nearly 115,000 jobs](https://www.semiconductors.org/chipping-away-assessing-and-addressing-the-labor-market-gap-facing-the-u-s-semiconductor-industry/). [It put about 67,000 at risk of going unfilled at current degree completion rates, 35 percent of them for engineers with four-year degrees or for computer scientists](https://www.semiconductors.org/chipping-away-assessing-and-addressing-the-labor-market-gap-facing-the-u-s-semiconductor-industry/).

To start, take your school's semiconductor devices course and any fab or cleanroom lab it offers.

## Applications engineering

Applications engineers help customers design a company's parts into the customers' own products, so you will explain circuits as much as you design them. At TI, [applications engineers solve customers' design challenges, which may involve designing printed circuit boards (PCBs), writing software, running simulations, and creating demos](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). [They also become experts on specific products and can publish technical content on TI.com](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). At SEL, [applications engineers work directly with customers to apply SEL products, and give them presentations and training](https://selinc.com/company/careers/engineer-rotation-program/).

[TI's Field Applications & Sales Training (FAST) rotation program has a field applications track that prepares you for a field applications engineer career](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering).

For a project, take a vendor's evaluation board, a demo board for one product, rebuild its reference design (the vendor's recommended circuit), and write up what you measured and debugged. The [[Forever learning#Read the part's documents|section on reading the part's documents]] on the forever learning page covers the application notes that go with a part.

Neither TI's page nor SEL's states a degree, so read each posting. The jobs sit at companies that sell parts and equipment to other engineers, such as TI for chips and SEL for grid protection. To start, take the courses for the path the company's products belong to, such as analog for amplifiers.

## Board-level hardware design

Board-level engineers turn a schematic into a printed circuit board, then power it on and debug it, so you hold and probe what you designed. [SEL's hardware engineers draw on circuit design, FPGA, analog design, and digital design](https://selinc.com/company/careers/hardware-engineering/). [They work on a project from conception to completion, since SEL designs, manufactures, and tests its devices on-site](https://selinc.com/company/careers/hardware-engineering/).

Search for electronics design engineer and design engineer, [two of O\*NET's reported titles for electronics engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00), and for hardware engineer.

For a project, follow the converter build in the [[Hardware#Hardware builds that count|hardware builds that count]] section on the hardware page, which takes one board from schematic to bench test. For courses, take the analog and digital courses above, plus any PCB design course your school offers.

Look for companies that design and build their own products, as SEL does. [The BLS counted computer and electronic product manufacturing at 10 percent of electrical engineer jobs in 2025, and transportation equipment manufacturing at 7 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

To start, [Altium gives students a free license for one year, renewable while you are a student, for Windows only](https://www.altium.com/education/students). [It also has a free college-level PCB design curriculum, which it says gives students real-world experience](https://www.altium.com/education/students). The [[Hardware#Starting on a student budget|section on starting on a student budget]] on the hardware page covers KiCad too.
