Most of this guide leans toward embedded work, because that is my field. [The BLS says electrical and electronics engineers may specialize in an area such as electric power, radio communications, or control systems](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). This page covers eleven paths. The first five are embedded, power, analog, RF, and controls. The other six sit mostly at chip and electronics companies: digital design, verification, test, chip process, applications, and board-level hardware. Each section says what the work is and who it suits. It then gives titles to search, a signal project, the coursework area, the usual degree, where the jobs are, and a place to start. License notes come last where they apply. Suggestions without a source are mine.

| Path                                      | Titles to search                                                                           | Degree usually expected                                        |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Embedded and firmware                     | Embedded software engineer, firmware                                                       | Bachelor's                                                     |
| Power                                     | Electrical engineer, electrical project engineer, utility rotation programs                | Bachelor's                                                     |
| Analog and mixed-signal                   | IC design engineer, product engineer                                                       | Bachelor's, often a master's for chip design                   |
| RF and communications                     | RF engineer                                                                                | Bachelor's, often more for RF chip design                      |
| Controls                                  | Electrical controls engineer, automation engineer                                          | Bachelor's                                                     |
| Digital design and FPGA                   | Hardware design engineer, electrical design engineer (FPGA/ASIC), physical design engineer | Bachelor's or master's                                         |
| Design verification                       | ASIC verification engineer, design verification engineer                                   | Bachelor's for internships, a master's on one new-grad posting |
| Test, validation, and product engineering | Test engineer, product engineer, validation engineer                                       | Bachelor's                                                     |
| Semiconductor process and yield           | Process integration engineer, yield enhancement engineer                                   | Bachelor's in EE, materials, or a related field                |
| Applications engineering                  | Applications engineer, field applications engineer                                         | Not stated on the TI or SEL pages                              |
| Board-level hardware design               | Electronics design engineer, hardware engineer                                             | Bachelor's                                                     |

For more on when a path needs a master's, read the [[Graduate study#Which fields expect one|section on which fields expect one]] on the graduate study page.

## How to choose and test a path

Test a path cheaply before you commit to it, and start with your courses. [Illinois ECE says students need to strike a balance between the breadth and depth of what they learn](https://ece.illinois.edu/academics/ugrad/subdisciplines). [It says students will likely take courses listed under several areas, and that a specialization is not recorded on the BS diploma](https://ece.illinois.edu/academics/ugrad/subdisciplines). So a wrong elective does not follow you onto your diploma.

Next, talk to engineers who do the work. [MIT's career office calls an informational interview a low-pressure way to gather career information that can help you refine your goals](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/conducting-informational-interviews/). [It warns against relying too heavily on the views of only one or two people](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/conducting-informational-interviews/), so talk to several in each path you weigh. The [[Networking#The informational interview|section on the informational interview]] on the networking page has how to ask for one.

An internship or co-op is the closest test to the real job, because you do the work for a summer or a semester. [UT Austin's Cockrell School lists exploring career interests and assessing interest in specific engineering fields among the benefits of an internship](https://cockrell.utexas.edu/student-life/career-services/co-ops-and-internships/). [A Drexel ECE student from the class of 2019 said co-op let them try out the fields and decide for sure what they liked](https://drexel.edu/engineering/academics/departments/electrical-computer-engineering/academic-programs/department-co-ops). [The BLS says employers also value practical experience, such as internships or cooperative engineering programs](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

After graduation, a rotation program keeps the test going with a salary. [GE Aerospace's Edison program has 3 to 4 rotations over 2 years](https://careers.geaerospace.com/global/en/edison-engineering-development-program). [Its rotations span hardware design, controls, validation testing, power electronics, and software](https://careers.geaerospace.com/global/en/edison-engineering-development-program). [Schweitzer Engineering Laboratories (SEL) says its 18-month rotation through three roles makes you a strong candidate for the engineering career path you choose](https://selinc.com/company/careers/engineer-rotation-program/). The power section below covers SEL's and PG&E's programs.

The closest data on switching later covers whole fields, not EE subfields. [In November 2016, SSTI reported on a study of people trained as scientists and engineers](https://ssti.org/blog/engineering-job-mismatch-spurs-study). [More than 40 percent worked outside their field of study or in a field only somewhat related, and most chose to](https://ssti.org/blog/engineering-job-mismatch-spurs-study). So treat your first job as one try of several.

Whichever path you test, the interview comes back to your resume and your courses. [Texas Instruments (TI) tells candidates to be prepared to discuss every detail on the resume, including tools, design steps, and results](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/recruitment-process). [It says technical questions may include coding problems, circuit analysis, or design scenarios](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/recruitment-process). [Micron says its technical questions typically focus on your completed coursework and problem-solving abilities](https://www.micron.com/about/careers/university-recruiting). So the electives you choose turn into interview material. The [[How to interview|how to interview page]] covers how to prepare, and some paths below add the topics one engineer or vendor lists for them.

## Embedded and firmware

Embedded engineers write firmware, the code that runs on a microcontroller, so the work sits where the circuit meets the software. [UT Austin's embedded systems design lab covers microcomputer-based embedded systems, with interfacing from both a hardware and a software perspective](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~valvano/EE445L/EE445L_SPRING_2025.htm). If you like reading a datasheet and then making the peripheral do what it says, this is the path.

Search for embedded software engineer, which is my own title. [One recruiter in aerospace and defense wrote that embedded software engineers are the hardest and most expensive candidates to find in that industry](https://www.reddit.com/r/EngineeringResumes/comments/1sj2rgt/student_landed_nvidia_offer_sophomore_cs_major_at/). Chip companies hire for it too. [TI's software engineers work on system architectures and embedded programming](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering), and [a 2022 list Purdue ECE posted of NVIDIA new-grad hardware roles included firmware](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ECE/Academics/Graduates/Announcements/20220408_NVIDIA_Jobs).

The [[Software#Embedded firmware|embedded firmware]] section on the software page covers the project, starting with bare-metal code on a microcontroller and then a real-time operating system. [Georgia Tech's ECE school files embedded systems and hardware-software codesign under VLSI systems and digital design](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

A bachelor's is the usual entry. [The BLS says electrical and electronics engineers typically need at least a bachelor's degree in a related engineering field](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). The [[Graduate study#Which fields expect one|graduate study page]] lists embedded among the fields that do not expect a master's. [The BLS says entry-level electrical and electronics engineer jobs do not require a license](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

For interviews, [a January 2026 Wind River blog post on embedded interviews asks about the heap and the stack, and about interrupt handlers](https://www.windriver.com/blog/Internal-Job-Interview-Questions-for-Embedded-Systems-Developers). [It also covers real-time operating system tasks, mutexes versus semaphores, priority inversion, and when to poll a register instead of using an interrupt](https://www.windriver.com/blog/Internal-Job-Interview-Questions-for-Embedded-Systems-Developers). [Wind River wrote the list for embedded developers looking for work, and says fundamentals questions are common when an applicant has little experience](https://www.windriver.com/blog/Internal-Job-Interview-Questions-for-Embedded-Systems-Developers). So start with the fundamentals. The [[How to interview#A note on embedded interviews|note on embedded interviews]] on the how to interview page shows how to trace one project from the pin to the code.

To start, [UT Austin's ECE 445L listed Embedded Systems: Real-Time Interfacing to ARM Cortex M Microcontrollers as its suggested text for Spring 2025](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~valvano/EE445L/EE445L_SPRING_2025.htm). [TI made its Precision Labs videos to accelerate the development of engineers early in their career](https://www.ti.com/video/series/precision-labs.html), which includes a new grad. [Its microcontroller series covers the CPU core, timers, memory, and programming](https://www.ti.com/video/series/precision-labs.html).

## Power

[O\*NET's tasks for electrical engineers include finding and correcting power system problems and tying wind, solar, and other renewable sources into electrical systems](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). [SEL's Engineering Services group gives customers expertise on power system dynamics, protection, automation, and control, from planning through commissioning](https://selinc.com/company/careers/engineer-rotation-program/). Power fits someone who liked three-phase circuits and wants work tied to real equipment, some of it in the field.

[O\*NET's reported titles for electrical engineers include electrical engineer, electrical design engineer, and electrical project engineer](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). Utilities also hire through rotation programs. [PG&E's Engineer Rotation Development Program in electric engineering gives 12 months in up to two departments, in Sacramento, Fresno, or the Bay Area](https://jobs.pge.com/university-programs). [SEL's Engineer Rotation Program runs 18 months through three areas of the company, such as research and development, engineering services, and manufacturing](https://selinc.com/company/careers/engineer-rotation-program/).

For a project, the converter build in the [[Hardware#Hardware builds that count|hardware builds that count]] section on the hardware page is power electronics. [Georgia Tech's ECE school files power system protection, power electronics, and motors, generators, and power conversion under electrical energy](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

A bachelor's is the usual entry. [PG&E says its ideal candidates are graduating with a bachelor's or master's in electrical engineering, and an emphasis in power systems is desired](https://jobs.pge.com/university-programs). Take a power systems elective if you can, because PG&E names that emphasis.

[The BLS counts engineering services as the largest employer of electrical engineers, at 22 percent, with electric power generation, transmission, and distribution at 10 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). [Schweitzer Engineering Laboratories offers year-round electrical engineering internships, many at its headquarters in Pullman, Washington](https://selinc.com/company/careers/sel-internships/).

[NCEES says the FE exam is generally the first step toward a PE license, and it designed the exam for recent graduates and students close to finishing an accredited engineering degree](https://ncees.org/exams/fe-exam/). [The PE Power exam is given year-round and is designed for engineers with at least four years of work after college](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/). [A Burns & McDonnell staff posting open in November 2026 put its substation protection engineer under a licensed professional engineer's supervision](https://burnsmcd.jobs/washington-dc/staff-substation-pc-engineer-transmission-distribution-mid-atlantic-region/7D486F7994A24FC7A3B6BE7F9643D31F/job/). [It asked for 3 years of experience, and listed a passed FE, an engineer in training (EIT) designation, or a PE as preferred](https://burnsmcd.jobs/washington-dc/staff-substation-pc-engineer-transmission-distribution-mid-atlantic-region/7D486F7994A24FC7A3B6BE7F9643D31F/job/). That is the job after a first job, so take the FE near graduation if this path is your goal.

To start, join the IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES). [Its student membership costs \$1.00 a year at the Essential level, and PES offers the first year free](https://ieee-pes.org/membership-overview/student-members/). [Student members get access to three PES scholarships, and to student support and poster contests at PES conferences](https://ieee-pes.org/membership-overview/student-members/). [MIT's 6.622 Power Electronics, from Spring 2023, is a graduate course with 6.2500 Nanoelectronics and Computing Systems as its prerequisite](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-622-power-electronics-spring-2023/pages/syllabus/). [Its text is Principles of Power Electronics, published by Cambridge University Press in 2023](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-622-power-electronics-spring-2023/pages/syllabus/). Save it for after your circuits and electronics courses.

## Analog and mixed-signal

[TI's design engineers in analog, digital, mixed-signal, and RF define, design, model, and verify integrated circuits](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). Analog work is for you if you like reasoning through a circuit one transistor at a time, then checking the answer on the bench. [O\*NET's reported titles for electronics engineers include integrated circuit design engineer, product engineer, and test engineer](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00).

[Analog Devices lists intern projects such as designing a high voltage, high output current operational amplifier and evaluating an on-chip buffer for a new analog-to-digital converter](https://www.analog.com/en/careers/student-resources.html). A student version is an op amp or ADC front end that you simulate in LTspice, build, and measure on the bench. [Georgia Tech's ECE school files analog electronics, high-speed mixed-signal systems, and power-management integrated circuits under electronic design and applications](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[In O\*NET's survey of electronics engineers, 83 percent said the job needs a bachelor's, and 3 percent a master's](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00). Chip design is the exception. The [[Graduate study#Which fields expect one|section on which fields expect one]] on the graduate study page covers the master's that analog IC design work often asks for.

[The BLS counts semiconductor and other electronic component manufacturing among the largest employers of electronics engineers, at 14 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). [Analog Devices says it hires over 500 interns and converts over 60 percent of them to full-time employees after graduation](https://www.analog.com/en/careers/student-resources.html).

The [[How to interview#Fundamentals from the ground up|fundamentals]] section on the how to interview page lists analog questions, such as op-amp gain and a buck converter versus a low-dropout regulator.

To start, [MIT's 6.002 Circuits and Electronics, from Spring 2007, is an undergraduate course designed as a first course in EE](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/). [Its text is Foundations of Analog and Digital Electronic Circuits](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/). [TI Precision Labs, which TI made for engineers early in their career, has series on amplifiers and on analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog converters](https://www.ti.com/video/series/precision-labs.html). [The IEEE Solid-State Circuits Society (SSCS) runs a Student Circuit Contest open to undergraduates, with no IEEE or SSCS membership needed](https://sscs.ieee.org/education/sscs-student-circuit-contest/). [Entries are a two-page answer from one student or a team of two, and up to three winners get \$500](https://sscs.ieee.org/education/sscs-student-circuit-contest/). [Every sound entry earns a free year of SSCS membership, and the 2026 deadline was May 31](https://sscs.ieee.org/education/sscs-student-circuit-contest/).

[The closest PE exam is Electronics, Controls, and Communications, given once a year](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).

## RF and communications

[Analog Devices lists intern projects such as moving an RF modulator design to a new process and writing software that automates the evaluation of voltage controlled oscillators](https://www.analog.com/en/careers/student-resources.html). Pick RF if electromagnetics was a course you enjoyed. [O\*NET lists radio frequency engineer among the reported titles for electronics engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00).

A ham radio license gets you transmitting before any internship. [The FCC says most new amateur radio operators start at the Technician class, after an exam given by volunteer examiners](https://www.fcc.gov/wireless/bureau-divisions/mobility-division/amateur-radio-service). [Georgia Tech's ECE school files antenna design, radar, communication, and navigation, and satellite communications under electromagnetics](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

The O\*NET degree figures in the analog section cover RF too. For RF chip design, the [[Graduate study#Which fields expect one|graduate study page]] quotes a commenter who said many RF design postings require a PhD.

[The BLS lists the federal government at 15 percent, telecommunications at 14 percent, and semiconductor manufacturing at 14 percent as the largest employers of electronics engineers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

[The unnamed author of antenna-theory.com, who has given and taken antenna engineer interviews, calls Smith charts, transmission lines, and impedance matching fair game](https://www.antenna-theory.com/careers/interview.php). [The sample questions ask you to match an impedance on a Smith chart with lumped parts, and to explain a quarter-wavelength transformer](https://www.antenna-theory.com/careers/interview.php). The page is written for antenna roles, so add your RF circuits course on top.

To start, [MIT's 6.013 Electromagnetics and Applications, from Spring 2009, is an undergraduate course with an open textbook and problem sets with solutions](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-013-electromagnetics-and-applications-spring-2009/). [Illinois's ECE 457, Microwave Devices and Circuits, assigned Pozar's Microwave Engineering, 4th edition, in Fall 2025](https://courses.grainger.illinois.edu/ece457/fa2025/). [It lists ECE 340 and ECE 350 as prerequisites](https://courses.grainger.illinois.edu/ece457/fa2025/), so treat Pozar as the book after electromagnetics. [The International Microwave Symposium (IMS) invites eligible students to its student design competitions, with cash prizes for the winning teams](https://ims-ieee.org/students). [Its Project Connect travel grants bring selected undergraduate and first-year graduate students to the conference](https://ims-ieee.org/students). The competition rules on that page refer to IMS 2026, so check the current year before you plan.

[The closest PE exam is Electronics, Controls, and Communications](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).

## Controls

[O\*NET's reported titles for electrical engineers include electrical controls engineer, and its tasks include developing software to control electrical systems](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). [It lists programmable logic controller (PLC) software and supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) software among the software electrical engineers use](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). If feedback and stability were the parts of class you liked, controls lets you see them on a machine.

[O\*NET's profile for mechatronics engineers adds automation engineer, control systems engineer, and process controls engineer as reported titles](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.05). [Its tasks include designing engineering systems that automate industrial tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.05).

A project that signals controls closes a loop on real hardware, such as a motor held at a set speed, and shows the step response you tuned it to. [TI Precision Labs, made for engineers early in their career, has a motor driver series on brushed-DC, stepper, and brushless-DC motors](https://www.ti.com/video/series/precision-labs.html). That series fits the project. [Georgia Tech's ECE school describes systems and controls as mathematical and computational techniques for modeling, estimation, and control](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[In O\*NET's survey of mechatronics engineers, 66 percent said the job needs a bachelor's](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.05). The [[Graduate study#Which fields expect one|graduate study page]] lists controls among the fields that do not expect a master's.

[O\*NET's top industries for electrical engineers are professional, scientific, and technical services, and manufacturing](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). The GE Aerospace rotation program above includes controls.

To start, [MIT's 6.302 Feedback Systems, from Spring 2007, is a graduate course on stability, root locus, the Nyquist criterion, and compensation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-302-feedback-systems-spring-2007/). Take your school's undergraduate controls course first, then use 6.302 to go further.

[NCEES gives a separate PE Control Systems exam, computer-based and offered once a year](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/control-systems/). The next one is April 14, 2027.

## Digital design and FPGA

Digital designers describe logic in a hardware description language (HDL), and tools turn that code into gates. [O\*NET's tasks for computer hardware engineers include designing CPUs and building and testing prototypes with computer simulation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2061.00). Digital design fits people who think in clock cycles and like code that turns into a circuit.

[A Micron new-grad posting in Boise, closed when checked in November 2026, had the engineer write HDL modules](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/30512424). [It also had them debug register-transfer level (RTL) code in simulation, then run synthesis, place and route, and static timing analysis](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/30512424). [The role built field-programmable gate array (FPGA) and application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC) designs for Micron's chip manufacturing test equipment](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/30512424).

[O\*NET's reported titles for computer hardware engineers include hardware design engineer, hardware engineer, and physical design engineer](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2061.00). [A 2022 list Purdue ECE posted of NVIDIA new-grad roles also named ASIC design and hardware design](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ECE/Academics/Graduates/Announcements/20220408_NVIDIA_Jobs).

[In MIT's 6.111, an undergraduate digital lab from Spring 2006, students build a final project of their choice in Verilog on an FPGA](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-111-introductory-digital-systems-laboratory-spring-2006/). A project like that signals this path, with the testbench shown next to the design. The [[Software#FPGA and HDL|section on FPGA and HDL]] on the software page lists free simulators. Alex's FPGA equalizer, under [[Hardware#Hardware builds that count|hardware builds that count]] on the hardware page, shows the resume lines.

[Georgia Tech's ECE school files computer architecture, and FPGAs and reconfigurable computing, under computer systems and software](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs). [NVIDIA's 2027 hardware ASIC design internship lists course or internship experience in RTL design, VLSI design, static timing analysis, and synthesis](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397025803).

[That internship takes students working toward a bachelor's, master's, or PhD in EE, computer engineering, or a related field](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397025803). [Micron's new-grad posting asked for a BS or MS in electrical engineering or a related discipline](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/30512424). [In O\*NET's survey of computer hardware engineers, 48 percent said the job needs a bachelor's and 34 percent a master's](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2061.00). That master's share is far above the 3 percent O\*NET reports for electronics engineers.

Physical design is the back half of the flow, where the logic becomes a layout of the chip. [NVIDIA's 2027 physical design internship lists floorplanning, place and route, static timing analysis, power integrity, physical verification, and clock tree synthesis](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397026661). [It takes the same bachelor's, master's, and PhD students](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397026661), and [Georgia Tech files design automation for physical design under VLSI systems and digital design](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs). It suits someone who likes tools and scripts as much as logic.

[The BLS counts semiconductor and other electronic component manufacturing as the largest employer of computer hardware engineers, at 24 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/computer-hardware-engineers.htm). [Research and development comes next at 18 percent, then computer systems design at 15 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/computer-hardware-engineers.htm). [The BLS put the May 2025 median pay for computer hardware engineers at \$161,740](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/computer-hardware-engineers.htm), against [\$125,040 for electrical and electronics engineers combined](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

[Bharath Suresh's Chip Insights newsletter tells digital design candidates to work out both a Mealy and a Moore model for any scenario](https://chipinsights.net/p/hardware-engineering-interview-resources). [It also lists the synthesizable Verilog constructs, setup and hold time, and metastability](https://chipinsights.net/p/hardware-engineering-interview-resources). [An unsigned topic list on The Data Bus, a chip design site, says every digital design interview is bound to have questions on counters](https://thedatabus.in/interview_list/). [It says a new graduate must know the basic clock domain crossing problem, even without having worked on it](https://thedatabus.in/interview_list/). Clock domain crossing is passing a signal between two clocks. The [[How to interview#Fundamentals from the ground up|fundamentals]] section on the how to interview page points to HDLBits for Verilog practice.

To start, take a course like 6.111 above. [The IEEE Circuits and Systems Society (CASS) Student Design Competition takes teams of 3 to 10 undergraduates with a faculty or graduate student mentor](https://ieee-cas.org/society-involvement/student-design-competition). [At least three team members must belong to CASS, and 2027 phase one registration runs from November 2, 2026, to January 11, 2027](https://ieee-cas.org/society-involvement/student-design-competition).

[The closest PE exam is Computer Engineering, given once a year and designed for engineers with at least four years of work after college](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/). [In the pass-rate table NCEES updated in January 2026, only 27 first-time takers sat it](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).

## Design verification

[Bharath Suresh's Chip Insights newsletter describes verification as writing tests for digital logic modules that cover all the different use cases](https://chipinsights.net/p/hardware-engineering-interview-resources). [NVIDIA's closed 2026 new-grad verification posting in Austin had the engineer turn the architecture specification into a test plan and carry it out](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893392585259). [The engineer would build Universal Verification Methodology (UVM) components, sequences, tests, and scoreboards, then launch regressions and resolve the issues](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893392585259). A regression is a full rerun of the test suite. It is a good fit if you like writing software and finding the bug someone else missed.

[A 2022 list Purdue ECE posted of NVIDIA new-grad roles included ASIC verification, formal verification, and GPU design verification](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ECE/Academics/Graduates/Announcements/20220408_NVIDIA_Jobs). Search for those titles and for design verification engineer.

For a project, take a small block of your own, such as a first-in, first-out (FIFO) buffer, and verify it. Write a test plan, add random tests, and report functional coverage, which measures how much of the plan the tests reached. The [[Software#FPGA and HDL|section on FPGA and HDL]] on the software page lists free tools for the testbench. [NVIDIA's 2027 ASIC design internship lists testbench creation, functional coverage, and object-oriented programming among the course areas it may require](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397025803).

The degree bar is where the sources disagree, so read each posting. [The NVIDIA new-grad posting asked for an MS or higher in computer or electrical engineering, or equivalent experience](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893392585259). [The NVIDIA ASIC design internship takes students working toward a bachelor's, master's, or PhD](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893397025803). [Micron's FPGA/ASIC posting from the digital design section, which included writing verification test plans, asked for a BS or MS](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/30512424). Verification jobs sit at the same chip companies as digital design, such as NVIDIA and Micron. So the BLS industry figures in that section apply here too.

To start, take the digital design course above, because a testbench checks a design you have to read first.

If the lab bench is where you like to work, start here.

So the signal project here is a lab script that runs an instrument and fails a part that is out of spec. ## Semiconductor process and yield

Process and yield engineers work where the chips are made. They find out why wafers and parts fail, and fix the cause. [TI's manufacturing engineers lead process optimization to meet fab cycle time goals and improve yield](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). It suits someone who liked device physics and statistics as much as circuits.

[A Micron new-grad process integration posting in Boise, closed when checked in November 2026, had the engineer own a process module](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [The engineer would run root cause investigations and design of experiments (DOE)](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [They would also use statistical process control (SPC) and failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [A second Micron new-grad posting, open in November 2026, has a yield enhancement engineer run electrical failure analysis and fault isolation](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42813179). [The failures come from production test, qualification, and customer returns of high bandwidth memory](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42813179).

[The process integration posting warned of a shifted schedule and international travel of 3 months or more for technology transfer](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [The yield posting mentions swing or night shifts](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42813179). Search for process integration engineer, yield enhancement engineer, and manufacturing engineer.

[The process integration posting listed familiarity with DOE, SPC, and JMP among its minimum qualifications](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). So a designed experiment on a real lab process, with the statistics done in JMP, signals this path.

[Georgia Tech's ECE school says its electronic design and applications area focuses on device and integrated circuit fabrication](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs). [The process integration posting preferred academic or internship exposure to transistors and metallization processes, yield ramp, and fabrication processes](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). The TI internship in the test section prefers coursework in semiconductor processing too.

[The process integration posting's minimum was a bachelor's in materials science, electrical, electronics, chemical, or a related engineering field](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [It preferred a master's or PhD and an internship in a 300 mm semiconductor environment](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42021708). [The yield posting takes a bachelor's or master's in EE, materials science, computer engineering, physics, or chemistry](https://careers.micron.com/careers/job/42813179). So these jobs are open to materials and chemistry majors as well as EE students.

[A 2023 Semiconductor Industry Association report projected nearly 115,000 new chip industry jobs by 2030, with about 67,000 at risk of going unfilled](https://www.semiconductors.org/chipping-away-assessing-and-addressing-the-labor-market-gap-facing-the-u-s-semiconductor-industry/). [It expects 35 percent of the unfilled jobs to be for engineers with four-year degrees or for computer scientists](https://www.semiconductors.org/chipping-away-assessing-and-addressing-the-labor-market-gap-facing-the-u-s-semiconductor-industry/).

To start, take your school's semiconductor devices course and any fab or cleanroom lab it offers.

## Applications engineering

Applications engineers help customers design a company's parts into the customers' own products. [TI's applications engineers solve customers' design challenges, and the solutions may involve designing printed circuit boards (PCBs), writing software, running simulations, and creating demos](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). [They also become experts on specific products and can publish technical content on TI.com](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering). [SEL's application engineers work directly with customers to apply SEL products, and give them presentations and training](https://selinc.com/company/careers/engineer-rotation-program/). You will explain circuits as much as you design them.

Search for applications engineer and field applications engineer. [TI's Field Applications & Sales Training (FAST) rotation program has a field applications track that prepares you for a field applications engineer career](https://careers.ti.com/en/sites/CX/pages/engineering).

For a project, take a vendor's evaluation board, rebuild its reference design, and write up what you measured and debugged. The [[Forever learning#Read the part's documents|section on reading the part's documents]] on the forever learning page covers the app notes that go with it.

Neither TI's page nor SEL's states a degree, so read each posting. These jobs sit at companies that sell parts and equipment to other engineers, such as TI for chips and SEL for grid protection. To start, use the course list for whichever path the vendor's parts belong to, such as the analog section for amplifiers.

## Board-level hardware design

Board-level engineers turn a schematic into a printed circuit board and bring it up on the bench. [SEL's hardware engineers draw on circuit design, FPGA, analog design, and digital design](https://selinc.com/company/careers/hardware-engineering/). [They work on a project from conception to completion, as SEL designs, manufactures, and tests its devices onsite](https://selinc.com/company/careers/hardware-engineering/). You get to hold the thing you designed and probe it.

[O\*NET's reported titles for electronics engineers include electronics design engineer and design engineer](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00). Search for hardware engineer too.

The converter build in the [[Hardware#Hardware builds that count|hardware builds that count]] section on the hardware page is the model. It takes one board from schematic to bench test. The coursework is the analog and digital courses above, plus whatever your school offers in PCB design.

The O\*NET survey in the analog section, where 83 percent of electronics engineers said the job needs a bachelor's, covers this work too. [The BLS counts computer and electronic product manufacturing at 10 percent of electrical engineer jobs, and transportation equipment manufacturing at 7 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). Look for companies that design and build their own products, as SEL does.

To start, [Altium gives students a free license for one year, renewable while you are a student, for Windows only](https://www.altium.com/education/students). [It also has a free college-level PCB design curriculum, which it says prepares students for real-world engineering careers](https://www.altium.com/education/students). The [[Hardware#Starting on a student budget|section on starting on a student budget]] on the hardware page covers KiCad too.
