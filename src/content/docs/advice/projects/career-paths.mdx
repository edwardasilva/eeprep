Most of this guide leans toward embedded work, because that is my field. [The BLS says electrical and electronics engineers may specialize in an area such as electric power, radio communications, or control systems](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). [It says employers value practical experience, such as internships or cooperative engineering programs](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

Below are five paths. Each gives titles to search, a project that signals the path, the topics behind it, and where the jobs are. The job postings I cite are single examples, and postings expire, so use them to learn the vocabulary. Suggestions without a source are mine.

## Embedded and firmware

Search for embedded software engineer, which is my own title. [One recruiter in aerospace and defense wrote that embedded software engineers are the hardest and most expensive candidates to find in that industry](https://www.reddit.com/r/EngineeringResumes/comments/1sj2rgt/student_landed_nvidia_offer_sophomore_cs_major_at/).

The [[Software#Embedded firmware|Embedded firmware]] section on the Software page covers the project. Start with bare-metal code on a microcontroller, then add a real-time operating system.

[Georgia Tech's ECE school files embedded systems and hardware-software codesign under VLSI systems and digital design](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[The BLS says entry-level electrical and electronics engineer jobs do not require a license](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

## Power

[O\*NET lists electrical engineer, electrical design engineer, electrical controls engineer, electrical project engineer, and test engineer as reported titles](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). [SEL posts power engineer intern roles in protection systems, where interns test technology and document results](https://selinc.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/SEL/job/Power-Engineer-Intern_2026-21156).

[In Illinois ECE 476, students write a load flow program for a sample system](https://ece.illinois.edu/academics/courses/ece476). The converter build in the [[Hardware#Hardware builds that count|Hardware builds that count]] section is power electronics.

[ECE 476 covers three-phase systems, per-unit notation, power flow, faults, and relaying](https://ece.illinois.edu/academics/courses/ece476). [ECE 464 covers dc-dc, ac-dc, dc-ac, and ac-ac converters, magnetics, and power semiconductor switches](https://ece.illinois.edu/academics/courses/ece464).

[The BLS puts engineering services first among employers of electrical engineers at 22 percent, and power generation, transmission, and distribution at 10 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

[NCEES calls the FE exam the first step toward a PE license, and designed it for students close to finishing an accredited degree](https://ncees.org/exams/fe-exam/). [The PE Power exam covers circuit analysis, power electronics, rotating machines, transmission and distribution, and protection](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/PE-Electrical_Power_Oct_2025.pdf). [The BLS says a PE license lets an engineer oversee other engineers and sign off on projects](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

## Analog and mixed-signal

[O\*NET lists integrated circuit design engineer, electronics design engineer, product engineer, test engineer, and evaluation engineer as reported titles](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00). [Analog Devices posts an associate analog design engineer role for 0 to 2 years of experience](https://analogdevices.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/External/job/Analog-Design-Engineer_R267037-1).

[The ADALM2000 is a USB-powered lab module for students](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/evaluation-hardware-and-software/evaluation-boards-kits/adalm2000.html). It works as an oscilloscope, function generator, logic analyzer, and network analyzer. [The Analog Devices posting calls internship or project experience in analog or mixed-signal design a plus](https://analogdevices.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/External/job/Analog-Design-Engineer_R267037-1).

[Berkeley EE 140 covers transistor amplifiers, op amps, feedback, data converters, and comparators, with a hardware lab and a design project](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Courses/EE140/). [The same posting asks for CMOS principles, an understanding of IC layout, and SPICE and Cadence Virtuoso](https://analogdevices.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/External/job/Analog-Design-Engineer_R267037-1).

[The BLS says semiconductor and other electronic component manufacturing employs 14 percent of electronics engineers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

## RF and communications

[O\*NET lists radio frequency engineer as a reported title for electronics engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00). [L3Harris posts an associate electrical engineer role with RF and antenna tracks, open to students and graduates from the last 12 months](https://jobs.tdworld.com/job/qved98/associate-electrical-engineering-%28rochester-ny%29/rochester/ny).

[IEEE MTT-S funds student chapters to buy nanoVNAs, which are open-source, handheld, 2-port vector network analyzers](https://mtt.org/nanovna-program/). [One project there used a nanoVNA and a sports field to measure antenna gain and patterns](https://mtt.org/nanovna-program/). A ham radio license gets you transmitting before any internship. [The FCC says most new amateur radio operators start at the Technician class, after an exam given by volunteer examiners](https://www.fcc.gov/wireless/bureau-divisions/mobility-division/amateur-radio-service).

[Berkeley EE 117 covers transmission lines, microwave networks, and antennas](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Courses/ELENG117/index.html). [Virginia Tech ECE 4104 adds S-parameters, the Smith chart, and impedance matching networks](https://ece.vt.edu/undergrad/courses/4104.html).

[The BLS lists the federal government at 15 percent and telecommunications at 14 percent of electronics engineers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

## Controls

[O\*NET's reported titles for electrical engineers include electrical controls engineer, and its tasks include developing software to control electrical systems](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). [It lists programmable logic controller (PLC) software and supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) software among the software electrical engineers use](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00).

A project that signals controls closes a loop on real hardware, such as a motor held at a set speed, and shows the step response you tuned it to.

[Georgia Tech's ECE school describes systems and controls as mathematical and computational techniques for modeling, estimation, and control](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[O\*NET's top industries for electrical engineers are professional, scientific, and technical services, and manufacturing](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00).
