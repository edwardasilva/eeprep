Most of this guide leans toward embedded work, because that is my field. [The BLS says electrical and electronics engineers may specialize in an area such as electric power, radio communications, or control systems](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). This page covers five paths. For each one it gives titles to search, a project that signals the path, the topics a school files under it, where the jobs are, and license notes. Suggestions without a source are mine.

## Embedded and firmware

Search for embedded software engineer, which is my own title. [One recruiter in aerospace and defense wrote that embedded software engineers are the hardest and most expensive candidates to find in that industry](https://www.reddit.com/r/EngineeringResumes/comments/1sj2rgt/student_landed_nvidia_offer_sophomore_cs_major_at/).

The [[Software#Embedded firmware|Embedded firmware]] section on the Software page covers the project, starting with bare-metal code on a microcontroller and then a real-time operating system.

[Georgia Tech's ECE school files embedded systems and hardware-software codesign under VLSI systems and digital design](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[The BLS says entry-level electrical and electronics engineer jobs do not require a license](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

## Power

[O\*NET's tasks for electrical engineers include finding and correcting power system problems and tying wind, solar, and other renewable sources into electrical systems](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). [Its reported titles include electrical engineer, electrical design engineer, and electrical project engineer](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00).

For a project, the converter build in the [[Hardware#Hardware builds that count|Hardware builds that count]] section on the Hardware page is power electronics.

[Georgia Tech's ECE school files power system protection, power electronics, and motors, generators, and power conversion under electrical energy](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[The BLS counts engineering services as the largest employer of electrical engineers, at 22 percent, with electric power generation, transmission, and distribution at 10 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). [Schweitzer Engineering Laboratories offers year-round electrical engineering internships, many at its headquarters in Pullman, Washington](https://selinc.com/company/careers/sel-internships/).

[NCEES says the FE exam is generally the first step toward a PE license, and it designed the exam for recent graduates and students close to finishing an accredited engineering degree](https://ncees.org/exams/fe-exam/). [The PE Power exam is given year-round and is designed for engineers with at least four years of work after college](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).

## Analog and mixed-signal

[O\*NET's reported titles for electronics engineers include integrated circuit design engineer, product engineer, and test engineer](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00).

[Analog Devices lists intern projects such as designing a high voltage, high output current operational amplifier and evaluating an on-chip buffer for a new analog-to-digital converter](https://www.analog.com/en/careers/student-resources.html). A student version is an op amp or ADC front end that you simulate in LTspice, build, and measure on the bench.

[Georgia Tech's ECE school files analog electronics, high-speed mixed-signal systems, and power-management integrated circuits under electronic design and applications](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[The BLS counts semiconductor and other electronic component manufacturing among the largest employers of electronics engineers, at 14 percent](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm). [Analog Devices says it hires over 500 interns and converts over 60 percent of them to full-time employees after graduation](https://www.analog.com/en/careers/student-resources.html).

[The closest PE exam is Electronics, Controls, and Communications, given once a year](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).

## RF and communications

[O\*NET lists radio frequency engineer among the reported titles for electronics engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00).

[Analog Devices lists intern projects such as moving an RF modulator design to a new process and writing software that automates the evaluation of voltage controlled oscillators](https://www.analog.com/en/careers/student-resources.html). A ham radio license gets you transmitting before any internship. [The FCC says most new amateur radio operators start at the Technician class, after an exam given by volunteer examiners](https://www.fcc.gov/wireless/bureau-divisions/mobility-division/amateur-radio-service).

[Georgia Tech's ECE school files antenna design, radar, communication, and navigation, and satellite communications under electromagnetics](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[The BLS lists the federal government at 15 percent, telecommunications at 14 percent, and semiconductor manufacturing at 14 percent as the largest employers of electronics engineers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm).

[The closest PE exam is Electronics, Controls, and Communications](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).

## Controls

[O\*NET's reported titles for electrical engineers include electrical controls engineer, and its tasks include developing software to control electrical systems](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00). [It lists programmable logic controller (PLC) software and supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) software among the software electrical engineers use](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00).

A project that signals controls closes a loop on real hardware, such as a motor held at a set speed, and shows the step response you tuned it to.

[Georgia Tech's ECE school describes systems and controls as mathematical and computational techniques for modeling, estimation, and control](https://ece.gatech.edu/research/tigs).

[O\*NET's top industries for electrical engineers are professional, scientific, and technical services, and manufacturing](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00).

[The closest PE exam is Electronics, Controls, and Communications](https://ncees.org/exams/pe-exam/electrical-and-computer/).
