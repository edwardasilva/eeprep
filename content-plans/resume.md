# Resume page outline

This file plans the eeprep.com resume page. The page is for electrical engineering students who want an internship or a first job. Edward Silva wrote the rules for friends on October 25, 2026. He tells friends to follow the r/EngineeringResumes wiki like gospel. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] He says he followed that wiki very closely. That habit got him past automated screeners. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Where his notes and the wiki disagree, the page follows his notes.

The wiki rules in this outline were checked against the January 5, 2024 text of that wiki. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] The only sample student is Alex Rivera. Do not name the friend in the October 25 notes, and do not print that friend's resume. A school, a GPA, a project, a metric, or a certification stays in a placeholder. One example is `[Alex persona: project 1 metric]`. The words come later from `sources/alex-rivera-persona.md`.

## Section order

The page uses five sections in Edward's order. They are the header, projects, experience, education, and skills. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Projects lead because Edward treats that section as the strongest proof a student has. For a student with no technical job, the wiki order is Education, then Projects, then Work Experience, then Skills. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Do not credit the wiki for Edward's order.

## Header

The header is the name plus one contact line. No horizontal rule sits under the name. Set the name flush left. Do not center it. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] The contact line holds six items. They are the phone with no label, the email, a plain LinkedIn path, the city, citizenship, and relocation. Edward's labels are "US citizen" and "Open to relocation". Print a label only when `sources/alex-rivera-persona.md` says it is true.

The sample puts Alex Rivera on the first line. Under the name, put the phone, the email, and the LinkedIn path, then the city, citizenship, and relocation. Phone is `[Alex persona: phone]`. Email is `[Alex persona: email]`. The LinkedIn path is `linkedin.com/in/[Alex persona: linkedin slug]`. City is `[Alex persona: city]`. Citizenship is `[Alex persona: citizenship]`. Relocation is `[Alex persona: relocation]`. Use the path `linkedin.com/in/` and use no logo. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] The wiki calls a phone number unnecessary. It also calls a LinkedIn URL unnecessary. It says to drop the city unless the job is in that city. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] The page still prints the phone, the LinkedIn path, and the city, as Edward does. Both sources ban a Phone label, and both ban an icon. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Write the URL with no `https://www.` prefix. Do not color the URL. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki]

## Why the page cuts the objective

Cut the objective. It takes lines and it says little. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] The wiki also cuts a summary for a student. A summary is allowed for a senior or staff engineer, for a career change, or for a return after an employment gap. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Alex Rivera is a student, so those saved lines go to projects.

## Projects

Projects take the most lines on the page. Put the tools on a line under the project title. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Repeat a tool in the Skills section when a bullet uses it. Edward wants that repeat on the title line. The wiki wants the same tools in the bullets and in Skills. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] A year-long date uses the form Fall 25 - Spring 26. The command `\hfill` pushes that date to the right margin. `\hfill` is the LaTeX command that does that push. The wiki also puts dates on the right margin. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki]

Write each bullet in XYZ form. XYZ means accomplished X as measured by Y by doing Z. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Edward's weak line is "Programmed and optimized drone flight path". His strong line is "Decreased flight path time by x% through a system architecture redesign". [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Read X as flight path time, Y as x%, and Z as the redesign. The wiki marks "programmed" as a weak verb and "decreased" as a strong verb. Move the number toward the start of the line. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] The wiki also names STAR and CAR. The page teaches XYZ because Edward uses that form.

The sample project invents nothing. Show four placeholders. The title is `[Alex persona: project 1 title]`. The tools are `[Alex persona: project 1 skills]`. The dates are `[Alex persona: project 1 dates]`. The result is `[Alex persona: project 1 metric]`. A bullet fits on one full line, or it fills a second line. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] A wrap of one to four words fails the test. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Name the section Projects. Do not call it Academic Projects. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Keep a job's project out of this section. The wiki keeps Projects for personal work, a student team, or a hobby build. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Do not put the word project in the title. End the bullet with no period. Start with a past-tense verb, and do not use I or we. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki]

## Experience

Name the section Experience. Edward rejects the name Work history. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] The wiki allows the name Experience, and it also allows Work Experience. It rejects Employment History and Job History. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Drop unrelated paid work. Edward's example of a cut is a retail job. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] The wiki limits this section to paid work, and research may sit here. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki]

Show a measured result. A task list with no number fails the test. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] The sample job is `[Alex persona: experience 1 title]`. The result slot is `[Alex persona: experience 1 result]`. Move serious team work into Experience. Edward's example of that move is a CubeSat team. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Alex's team slot is `[Alex persona: team experience title]`. If a hobby remains, name that block Other interests or Volunteer experience. Cut the line when it is vague. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Do not use the heading Extracurricular activities and interests.

## Education

Place Education under Experience and above Skills. The degree line is `[Alex persona: degree]`. The school line under it is `[Alex persona: school]`. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Put `[Alex persona: GPA]` on the same line as the major. Print a GPA only when it is above 3.75, and keep two decimal places. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Do not invent a number for Alex to pass that test.

Add a line named Relevant coursework. Bold that label on the resume. Edward adds the line because recruiters liked it. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] The wiki allows a course only when it is highly specialized. Its example course is Underwater Autonomous Robotics. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] The page keeps Edward's line and limits it to specialized courses. Alex's courses stay in `[Alex persona: coursework]`. Edward's example of a coursework topic is orbital mechanics. He pulls that topic out of Skills when it is only "knowledge". [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice]

Add an Activities line under coursework. Edward's sample entries are CubeSat and Tau Beta Pi. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Alex's entries stay in `[Alex persona: activities]`. Leave stray periods off the degree line and the school line. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] The wiki's date for a current student is the example Expected May 2025. Alex's date stays in `[Alex persona: graduation date]`. The wiki prints the graduation month and year, and it skips the start date. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Do not list a high school. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki]

## Skills

Name the section Skills. Keep one column and use at most three lines. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Edward keeps about two lines. The saved lines go to projects and experience. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Aim for two lines. Do not go past three lines. Do not use two columns. Edward says a two-column list looks like hiding thin experience. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice]

Order the entries from most important to least important. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Label the lines. Edward's labels are Software and Certifications. His format example is "Certifications: CSWP" (Certified SolidWorks Professional). [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] CSWP stays on Edward's sample line. Alex's certificate stays in the placeholder below. Separate names with commas. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] The Software slot is `[Alex persona: software skills]`. The Certifications slot is `[Alex persona: certifications]`. Omit the Certifications line when the persona lists none.

Cut soft skills. Edward's cuts are communication and problem solving. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] The wiki makes the same cut for teamwork and leadership. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Drop the word knowledge unless a certificate backs it. Do not write "Expert in" before a tool. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki]

## Dates and spacing

Keep one gap between sections. Set that gap with `\vspace` and reuse the value. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] `\vspace` is the LaTeX length for the gap between sections. A bullet uses one full line or two full lines. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Too much white space in the body is a red flag, so put leftover lines on the strongest project. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice]

Keep each margin at or above 0.5 inch. Keep the line spacing at or above 1.07, the floor the wiki gives. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Do not shrink a margin to buy a line. A student resume is one page. The wiki allows a second page after about 10 years of work. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki]

Set the text flush left. Do not center a line. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] Do not stretch a line so both margins are full. The wiki rejects that stretch because the gaps between words jump. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] For a job that is still open, write Present. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki] Class dates keep Edward's form, Fall 25 - Spring 26. The wiki rejects a season and a short year. Its example runs from Mar 2012 to Mar 2022, with an en dash and a space on each side. [@reddit-engineeringresumes-wiki]

## LaTeX commands

Add `\pdfgentounicode=1` in the preamble. The command maps characters to Unicode so a parser can read the text. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] An application tracking system (ATS) is software that reads a resume before a person does. Edward adds the command so the ATS gets Unicode text. The January 5, 2024 wiki text does not mention this command. Do not cite the wiki for it.

Add `\hypersetup` in the preamble too. [@edward-silva-2026-resume-advice] The command writes the PDF properties. Treat that command as a cheap extra. Anyone can open the PDF properties and read the fields. Do not present the fields as a hidden edge. Issue #7 picks the exact fields inside the command.

## Checklist

The page closes with a checklist for a printed draft. Each item is one instruction. The list has 10 items.

1. Order the sections as header, projects, experience, education, then skills.
2. Use one contact line and no rule under the name.
3. Cut the objective.
4. Put the tools under each project title.
5. Write each bullet in XYZ form and keep the number in `[Alex persona: project 1 metric]`.
6. Push each project date to the right margin with `\hfill`.
7. Keep Skills to one column and to at most three lines.
8. Add `\pdfgentounicode=1` so a parser can read Unicode text.
9. Write the PDF properties with `\hypersetup`, then open them and read them.
10. Fill the Alex Rivera sample from `sources/alex-rivera-persona.md` before the page ships.
